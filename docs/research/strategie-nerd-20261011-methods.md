## Scope and method

This pass covers 49 groups. Public developer/publisher product pages were searched; 44 have a source URL and 5 remain explicitly unknown. Steam page descriptions are publisher/developer claims, not a full gameplay review. No videos, media, captions, or transcripts were downloaded or saved.

Usefulness is judged by transfer to simple GradeCrew learning games: readable visual response, clear cause and effect, ease of simplifying, and low integration burden. Whole-game complexity is not a fitness criterion. Asset provenance and permission remain unknown for every title; commercial game assets are not reusable by default.

Current provisional scoring: 44 of 49 labels have a usefulness score; the five labels without verified official source access remain unscored. Both Die Gilde labels point to Steam app 2977260 and are aliases in one product family, not two confirmed unique games.

## Mechanic families with simple transfer ideas

- **seasonal feedback** (Manor Lords): Show a small season change tied to a visible town state.
- **visual identity** (Wild West Pioneers): Use only themed palette/story cues; create original assets.
- **cause/effect feedback** (Oxygen Not Included): Show one variable changing through a clear status color and cause/effect action.
- **construction feedback** (Aquapark Tycoon): A small attraction-building task is plausible; exact gameplay remains unverified.
- **material transformation** (Sandustry): Show one object transforming through three visible steps using original shapes.
- **task/status feedback** (Global Rescue): One incident card with type, location, state and resolution cue.
- **environmental/accessibility feedback** (Life Below): Show before/after ecosystem state and offer contrast/text size controls.
- **character reaction** (Sintopia): Distinctive character reactions can enliven a repeated task; original characters.
- **role feedback** (Soulmask): Simplify to two learner roles: do vs delegate.
- **balance feedback** (Airborne Empire): A balance marker with immediate stabilizing response can teach spatial reasoning.
- **process sequence** (Alchemy Factory): Animate order-to-product sequence with clear completion signal.
- **route feedback** (City Bus Manager): Route preview plus one small metric before/after.
- **character task feedback** (Colonus): Show one assigned task, character action, and result; create original art.
- **choice feedback** (Console War Giant): Short phases with consequence preview support unhurried learning choices.
- **chain reaction** (Fogpiercer): Small-grid puzzle with target outlines and one controlled reaction.
- **automation/result feedback** (Graveyard Keeper 2): One production loop, worker cue and changing inventory count; original art.
- **agents/accessibility** (Imperial Ambitions): Text/contrast controls transfer directly; simplify agents to visible destinations.
- **production feedback** (Kaiserpunk): Show one city action feeding one visible output.
- **perspective feedback** (Masters of Albion): One mode toggle between arranging and inspecting scene; clear transition.
- **world-state visualization** (Plan B: Terraform): Show a simplified world gradient as learner adds water/trees; no climate simulation.
- **goal feedback** (Possible One: Lunar Industries): Connect one science choice to resource delivery and an explicit goal check.
- **interactive reference** (Realindustry): Small optional “why this works” reference card, curriculum-checked.
- **shape feedback** (Shapez 2): Shape/color transformations teach sequences without resource economy.
- **energy visualization** (Solarpunk): Tiny circuit with stored→connected→lit state.
- **layered spatial view** (Space Drilling Station): Cutaway shows one resource moving between two floors.
- **task feedback** (Star Trek: Outposts Unknown): One task, one character, one visible outcome.
- **modular construction** (Steel Artery: Train City Builder): Three-car toy train with carriage placement preview.
- **route/landmark feedback** (SubwaySim 2): Route-following task with next stop and one action cue.
- **scenario comparison** (Terra Invicta: Dark Skies): Compare two short scenarios with different starting conditions.
- **environment cues** (Valheim): Distinct biome palettes/landmarks make a learning space legible.
- **choice consequence** (War Angels): Only neutral, low-stakes branching feedback is transferable; topic itself is too serious.

## 3D-, 2D- und UI-Methoden mit offiziellen Grundlagen

Die transferierbaren Ideen sind zunächst Informations- und Feedbackmuster. Für einfache 2D-Lernspiele genügt eine klare Zustandsänderung mit eigenem Icon/Illustration; sie braucht keine 3D-Rekonstruktion. Für Web-3D beschreibt three.js Geometrie als `BufferGeometry` und verbindet sie mit Material im `Mesh`; das ist ein passender, leichter Weg für eine kleine interaktive Lernszene. Blender dokumentiert UV-Texturierung als Abbildung der Modelloberfläche auf ein 2D-Bild. Für bestehende Unreal-Arbeit nennt Epic den FBX-Importweg, Materialien, Kollision und LODs; ein importiertes Mesh muss im Static Mesh Editor geprüft werden. Diese Wege ergänzen vorhandene GradeCrew-Entscheidungen, begründen keinen Enginewechsel. ([three.js Mesh](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Mesh.html), [three.js BufferGeometry](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/BufferGeometry.html), [Blender UV Manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/de/5.2/modeling/meshes/uv/applying_image.html), [Unreal FBX Static Mesh Pipeline](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/fbx-static-mesh-pipeline-in-unreal-engine), [Unreal Static Meshes](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/static-meshes))

Die aktuelle GradeCrew-Regel [IMG2THREEJS_AND_ASSET_QUALITY.md](https://github.com/HerrLoeffler/Hausaufgabe/blob/main/docs/games/IMG2THREEJS_AND_ASSET_QUALITY.md) verlangt bei passenden 3D-Objekt-/Figurenrekonstruktionen das vorhandene img2threejs v2.0.0 Skill-Verfahren inklusive Mehrwinkelrender und Vorlagenvergleich. Three.js-Code ist kein direkt importierbares Unreal-Asset; Rig und Bewegung brauchen eigene Prüfung. Für reine UI/2D/Gameplay-Lernlogik gibt es keinen 3D-Zwang. Assetreferenz und Nutzungsrechte müssen separat belegt werden.
