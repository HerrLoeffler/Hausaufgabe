Reference image supplies only the soft, charming 3D storybook material and lighting style. Exact layout: 3 columns by 2 rows, six equal rectangular cells, precisely aligned centers, ample transparent gutters and margin; every subject fits entirely inside its cell, no crossover. Top row: left a cream bunny wearing a clearly RED scarf; middle the exact same cream bunny pose wearing a clearly BLUE scarf; right the exact same cream bunny pose wearing a clearly YELLOW scarf. All animals smile naturally, child-friendly but polished. The three bunnies must have the same scale, face, pose and cream fur; only scarf colour changes. Consistent three-dimensional soft fur/feather/material, warm neutral lighting, easy to recognise colour differences. Landscape sheet, exact 3x2 grid.
