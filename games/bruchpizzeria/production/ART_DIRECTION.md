# Bruchpizzeria Bildstil und Spielabschnitt

GC-GAMES-PIZZA-01, 07.10.2026. Compact friendly indie kitchen inspired by the user's two restaurant screenshots, with original assets.

Warm butter-yellow tile, terracotta brick, basil green trim, cream counters and wood cutting board. Slightly raised walls and counters give depth to an overhead room. Large round-headed chef with white toque and green apron; guests with distinct clothes and expressions. No emojis as character assets. Oven glow, basil plants, plates, menu chalkboard and striped awning provide a coherent restaurant.

Interaction path: oven → cutting board → guest counter. Chef visibly carries pizza or plate. The cutting view shows a large whole pizza, actual cuts and selected slices; instructions and order stay visible. Friendly guests greet, wait and thank. First guided order is 1/2, later 1/4 and 3/4; accepting 2/4 for 1/2 teaches equivalence.

Player controls: click/tap destination or station; arrows/WASD and E; HTML station buttons provide accessible alternatives. Drawing a cut uses mouse/finger. Angle slider and “Schnitt setzen” give keyboard users the same centre-line cut, never an automatic correct-answer button. Undo is available. Incorrect geometry prompts “Die Teile sind noch unterschiedlich groß”.

Single-player browser practice, one scene, four orders and at most two waiting guests. Quiet initial instruction, then mild patience. Help/pause freezes learning and timers. Warm restrained sounds after interaction, default off. Reduced motion suppresses decorative motion. Text and shape reinforce colors.
